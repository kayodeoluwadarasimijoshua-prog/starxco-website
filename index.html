Who we are

A facilitator earns its place in the middle of a transaction

Deals stall when parties cannot find each other, cannot verify each other, or cannot keep the paperwork moving. StarXCO exists to remove those three failures — introducing credible counterparties, keeping communication orderly, and coordinating the documentation a transaction needs to proceed.

  • We introduce and coordinate — the parties themselves agree commercial terms and contract directly.
  • We take no title to goods and no possession of cargo, vessels or funds — unless a specific transaction is separately structured that way.
  • Every requirement is subject to availability, verification and applicable regulations.

Our process

How we work

Seven steps take a requirement from first contact to execution. You set out what you need; StarXCO coordinates the parties and the paperwork that follow.

See the full process
  1. 01

    Requirement

    You provide the product, asset or service requirement you want to fulfil.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    StarXCO reviews the requirement and identifies realistic ways to meet it.

  3. 03

    Sourcing

    Suitable suppliers, buyers, vessels, trucks, logistics providers or other counterparties are identified.

  4. 04

    Coordination

    StarXCO facilitates communication between the relevant parties.

  5. 05

    Commercial terms

    The parties discuss and agree the applicable commercial terms.

  6. 06

    Documentation

    The relevant transaction and supporting documentation is coordinated.

  7. 07

    Execution

    The transaction, shipment, charter, delivery or service proceeds on the agreed terms.
