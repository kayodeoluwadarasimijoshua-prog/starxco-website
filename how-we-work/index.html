Our process

Seven steps, requirement to execution

The same disciplined route for every requirement — a product to source, a vessel to charter, cargo to move or a supplier to find. You set out what you need; StarXCO coordinates the parties and the paperwork that follow.

The route

What happens at each step

No step is skipped and no introduction is made before verification. That is what keeps a StarXCO-facilated transaction defensible for every party involved.

  1. 01

    Requirement

    You provide the product, asset or service requirement you want to fulfil — specification, volume, origin or destination, and target timing where known.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    StarXCO reviews the requirement and identifies realistic ways to meet it, including the documents, verifications and logistics it will involve.

  3. 03

    Sourcing

    Suitable suppliers, buyers, vessels, trucks, logistics providers or other counterparties are identified from our network and the wider market.

  4. 04

    Coordination

    StarXCO facilitates communication between the relevant parties, keeping positions, questions and schedules in one orderly channel.

  5. 05

    Commercial terms

    The parties discuss and agree the applicable commercial terms directly — price, quantity, delivery basis, inspection and payment structure.

  6. 06

    Documentation

    The relevant transaction and supporting documentation is coordinated: commercial, product and maritime papers tracked through to issue.

  7. 07

    Execution

    The transaction, shipment, charter, delivery or service proceeds on the agreed terms, with StarXCO coordinating until it is complete.

Division of work

What you provide, what we handle

From you

A clear requirement: product or asset, specification and quantity, origin/destination, target dates, and the authority to proceed. Company details for verification.

From StarXCO

Assessment, counterparty sourcing and verification, introductions, communication coordination, documentation tracking and follow-through to execution.

Between the parties

Commercial terms, contracts and payment. StarXCO facilitates and coordinates; the principals negotiate and contract directly with each other.

Throughout

Confidentiality of your requirement and counterparty identities until both sides agree to be introduced, and honesty about what is and is not achievable.

Typical first response within one business day. Complex requirements may take longer to assess before an introduction is proposed.
