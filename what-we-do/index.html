Sector detail

Commodity facilitation

Commodity deals fail on trust and paperwork more often than on price. StarXCO connects buyers and sellers whose positions are real and documented, then keeps the process orderly through to execution.

  • Buyer and seller verification before introduction, including mandate and authority checks.
  • Coordination of commercial documents — from expression of interest through to contract and invoice.
  • Product documentation coordination: specifications, certificates of origin and quality, inspection where required.
  • Logistics and delivery alignment so product, fleet and terminal schedules match the agreed terms.

Sector detail

Trade & procurement

For buyers who need a supplier found and vetted, and suppliers who need a serious buyer reached, StarXCO coordinates the commercial path between them across international trade.

  • Supplier sourcing and vetting against the buyer’s specification, volume and target market.
  • Procurement coordination from requirement through quotation, terms and documentation.
  • Buyer-to-seller communication kept structured, so positions and terms stay clear.
  • Alignment with freight, chartering or haulage where the trade requires movement.
Every opportunity is subject to availability, counterparty verification and applicable regulations.
