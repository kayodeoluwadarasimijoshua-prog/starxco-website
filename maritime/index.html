Maritime & vessel chartering

Vessel chartering, sourcing and maritime support across tankers, bulk carriers, container and general cargo vessels — matching tonnage to cargo, and owners to charterers, with the marine services a call requires.

Vessel chartering

Coordinating chartering enquiries between charterers and owners or operators — voyage, time or bareboat — through to fixture coordination on agreed terms.

Vessel sourcing

Identifying suitable, available tonnage for a cargo: right size, right class, right position, right laycan — verified before it is presented.

Cargo placement

For owners and operators with open tonnage: reaching credible charterers and cargo interests whose requirements match the vessel’s position.

Bunkering & marine support

Coordination of marine fuel supply and marine services around vessel calls, aligned with port and terminal schedules.

Port & terminal coordination

Liaising with ports, terminals and cargo-handling facilities so berths, laytime and cargo operations match the charter terms.

Maritime documentation

Coordination of charter parties, notices of readiness, bills of lading, manifests and port clearance documents between the parties.

Chartering, coordinated

Tonnage and cargo, matched properly

A chartering enquiry is only as good as the verification behind it. StarXCO checks the cargo interest and the vessel interest before either is introduced, then keeps the negotiation orderly through recap and documentation.

  • Cargo and tonnage positions verified before introduction.
  • Terms negotiated directly between charterer and owner; StarXCO coordinates.
  • Recap, charter party and voyage documentation tracked through to completion.
